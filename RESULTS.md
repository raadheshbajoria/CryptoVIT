## How to read this

* Chance level for 7 balanced classes is 14.3% balanced accuracy.
* Balanced accuracy is the mean of the per-class sensitivities; plain accuracy is lower for the weighted models because the loss favours the rare classes.
* Rare classes have few test images (23-29), so one run's balanced accuracy can move by several points with another seed: differences of a few points are within noise.
* The block scheme gives only visual protection (no diffusion, colour histogram preserved); the per-pixel scheme scrambles far more but is much harder to learn from.
* Research prototype; not a medical device; not for clinical use.
